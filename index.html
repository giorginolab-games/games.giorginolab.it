About the research

From computational models
to playable questions.

GiorginoLab develops computational models of biomolecular motion and interactions, drawing on molecular dynamics, statistical learning and software engineering. The lab is led by Toni Giorgino at CNR-IBF, hosted by the Department of Biosciences at the University of Milan.

This new direction asks how games can make the behaviour of models something we can explore directly. A simulation becomes a place to try an idea, observe a result and develop intuition.

The collection begins with quantum puzzles, physical systems, electrophysiology and geometric patterns. We are exploring how interaction with these models can support scientific intuition.
